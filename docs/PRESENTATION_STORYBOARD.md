# Presentation storyboard

The canonical Prompt-5 narrative, English slide copy, Romanian speaker notes, visual guidance,
appendix references, objections, and truthful answers are maintained in
[`EXECUTIVE_PRESENTATION_PACKAGE.md`](EXECUTIVE_PRESENTATION_PACKAGE.md).

## Non-negotiable rules

- Generate every displayed number from one explicitly selected certified release.
- Never type or maintain a presentation KPI independently in this storyboard.
- Put the persistent synthetic-data statement and release identity on every exported artifact.
- Show exact numerator and denominator with every material rate.
- Treat configured scenario differences as assumptions, never market rankings.
- Display model evidence only when it is release-matched and verified; otherwise show unavailable.
- Use Presentation Mode and the deterministic sequence in
  [`FIVE_MINUTE_DEMO.md`](FIVE_MINUTE_DEMO.md).

## Canonical nine-part narrative

1. A confident denominator beats a confident prediction.
2. Build the analytical contract before real-data onboarding.
3. One longitudinal journey, not disconnected tables.
4. Every number has a denominator.
5. Longitudinal answers change when follow-up or definitions change.
6. Every result opens to method, limitation, and evidence.
7. Weak or unavailable models show us where not to automate.
8. From synthetic evidence engineering to a governed pilot.
9. Pilot one question, not an entire platform.
