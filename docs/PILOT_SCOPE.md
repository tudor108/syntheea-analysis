### In scope

- one prespecified descriptive, longitudinal, or model-evaluation question;
- approved aggregate outputs and minimum necessary source fields;
- denominator, timing, censoring, missingness, and source-lineage validation;
- comparison with an approved baseline or current workflow where relevant;
- human review, harm monitoring, and documented stop criteria.

### Out of scope

- treatment recommendations, patient-level actions, autonomous decisions, or care substitution;
- causal or comparative-effectiveness claims without a separate approved design;
- provider/site/market ranking or commercial performance assessment;
- population prevalence, incidence, market sizing, or extrapolation from synthetic values;
- model deployment, automated outreach, or production aliases;
- reuse outside the approved source, purpose, population, region, or retention period.

## Minimum deliverables

1. approved protocol or analysis plan;
2. source fit-for-use record and data contract;
3. cohort and denominator reconciliation;
4. aggregate result with uncertainty appropriate to the method;
5. limitations, harms, and subgroup support;
6. reproducible evidence manifest;
7. decision memo signed by named reviewers.
