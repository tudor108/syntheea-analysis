# Pilot decision charter

> Synthetic analytical planning artifact. It is not Bayer, patient, clinical, RWE, commercial, or
> causal evidence and does not authorize an operational workflow.

## Decision to be prepared

Should one narrowly defined analytical question proceed to a governed feasibility pilot using an
approved source, population, workflow, method, and human decision owner?

## Required identifiers

- TARGET MARKET: **TO BE AGREED**
- TARGET DATA SOURCE: **TO BE AGREED**
- BAYER WORKFLOW OWNER: **TO BE AGREED**
- CLINICAL/RWE OWNER: **TO BE AGREED**
- STATISTICAL OWNER: **TO BE AGREED**
- PRIVACY/SECURITY OWNER: **TO BE AGREED**
- DECISION DATE: **TO BE AGREED**

## Charter rules

1. Pilot one question, not the complete platform.
2. Define population, numerator, denominator, time zero, horizon, censoring, competing events,
   missingness, estimator, and source fields before analysis.
3. Record the workflow decision being informed and the human who remains accountable.
4. Prespecify validation, potential harms, stop criteria, and escalation.
5. Do not transfer synthetic rates, market differences, or model performance into expectations for
   real data.

## Entry evidence

- approved pilot question and analysis contract;
- completed source fit-for-use assessment;
- privacy, security, legal, source-owner, clinical/RWE, and statistical approvals;
- confirmed regional, retention, access, encryption, and audit controls;
- baseline workflow and comparison strategy;
- capacity, human oversight, incident, and rollback plan.

## Decision options

- **GO TO CONTROLLED FEASIBILITY:** all entry gates approved; no operational or clinical action.
- **CONDITIONAL GO:** named conditions, owners, due dates, and stop criteria recorded.
- **NO-GO:** source, method, governance, safety, or workflow evidence is insufficient.

## Success and stop conditions

Success criteria and thresholds are **TO BE AGREED** before data access. Stop immediately on an
unresolved data-contract breach, denominator mismatch, privacy/security incident, invalid endpoint,
material subgroup harm, unapproved use, or inability to preserve human oversight.
