# Stage-4 decision memo template

## Decision summary

- CANDIDATE / PILOT ID: **TO BE AGREED**
- DECISION: **GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO — TO BE AGREED**
- DATE: **TO BE AGREED**
- ACCOUNTABLE DECISION OWNER: **TO BE AGREED**
- CERTIFIED RELEASE / EVIDENCE PACKAGE: **TO BE AGREED**

## Question and intended use

State one analytical question, the human workflow decision it may inform, and every prohibited use.
Do not describe an exploratory synthetic result as a real-data opportunity.

## Evidence reviewed

- source fit-for-use and approvals;
- population, numerator, denominator, time zero, horizon, censoring, and missingness contract;
- data-quality and denominator reconciliation;
- analytical result and appropriate uncertainty;
- subgroup support and potential harms;
- model disposition and validation, if relevant;
- security, privacy, accessibility, operational capacity, incident, and rollback evidence;
- release manifest, source/configuration identity, and evidence hashes.

## Findings and limitations

Document exact n/N, method, uncertainty type, missing evidence, external-validity limits, and the
synthetic-to-real transition risk. Record dissenting reviews verbatim or by linked reference.
