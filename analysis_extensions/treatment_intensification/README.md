# Treatment Intensification Analysis

## Question

Among patients in the governed synthetic `mhspc_mcspc` pathway, how often is treatment initiated, and how often does treatment become scenario-supported intensified treatment by 30, 60, and 90 days?

## Definitions

- **Population:** the existing governed `mhspc_mcspc` cohort from `outputs/eda/cohort_patient_flags.parquet`.
- **Denominator:** governed eligible patients evaluable under the existing censor-aware `observable_for_30d`, `observable_for_60d`, and `observable_for_90d` fields.
- **Any treatment initiation:** the existing normalized treatment initiation fields from the governed cohort output.
- **Synthetic intensification:** the earliest linked treatment component on or after the governed cohort index, before or on censoring, where `drug_class` is `ARPI` or `chemotherapy` and the component belongs to an observed post-index treatment episode. ADT monotherapy is not intensified.
- **Timing source:** `treatment_regimen_component.component_start_date`; `treatment_regimen.intensification_flag` is used only for reconciliation.
- **Landmark states:** `UNTREATED`, `TREATED_NOT_INTENSIFIED`, and `INTENSIFIED`.

## Inputs

- `outputs/eda/cohort_patient_flags.parquet`; this must be generated by the existing cohort EDA first.
- Certified `treatment_episode.parquet`, `treatment_regimen.parquet`, and `treatment_regimen_component.parquet`, resolved through the repository's existing analytical-dataset resolver.

## Run

From the repository root, run:

```text
python analysis_extensions/treatment_intensification/treatment_intensification_analysis.py
```

The script fails clearly when the governed cohort output is missing and fails validation when contract invariants are violated. It does not modify upstream data or EDA outputs.

## Outputs

All outputs are aggregate files under `analysis_extensions/treatment_intensification/outputs/`:

- `intensification_funnel.csv`: overall 30/60/90-day KPIs;
- `intensification_by_segment.csv`: overall, market, and initial-care-setting KPIs;
- `intensification_timing.csv`: index-to-intensification and delayed add-on treatment-to-intensification summaries;
- `intensification_reconciliation.csv`: date, lineage, category, initiation, and regimen-flag checks;
- `intensification_summary.md`: concise results and limitations.

## Interpretation and Limitations

Rates use the evaluable eligible denominator at each landmark. Timing statistics use only evaluable patients with an observable component-derived intensification date. Component-derived status is used for timing; mismatches with the regimen-level flag are reported rather than silently corrected.

The analysis is limited to the governed mHSPC pathway and three interpretable segment views. All data are synthetic, and all findings are exploratory and descriptive. The results support no causal claims, clinical recommendations, real-world market-size claims, or invented financial value. Use `synthetic intensification` or `scenario-supported intensification` rather than treating the rule as clinical guidance.
